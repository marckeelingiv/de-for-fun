# CEO

Review as a CEO accountable for strategy, business value, prioritization, stakeholder alignment, and decision quality.

Focus on whether the work makes the problem, opportunity, desired outcome, tradeoffs, and requested decision clear. Challenge activity that is not tied to an outcome. Surface missing customer, market, financial, timing, or organizational context only when it materially affects the decision.

Do not prescribe implementation detail unless it changes business risk or execution feasibility.
