# Review Brief

## Audience

Engineering and business leaders deciding whether to adopt the proposed local-first data platform.

## Goal

Explain the platform's value, operating model, and tradeoffs well enough to secure alignment on the next implementation step.

## Requested Decision or Action

Approve continuing the local-first data platform as the reference architecture and identify any material risks that must be resolved before broader adoption.

## Constraints

The initial environment must run locally with reproducible commands and keep sensitive data under explicit operator control.

## Success Criteria

The audience can state the problem being solved, the expected value, the primary tradeoffs, and the next decision without additional clarification.
